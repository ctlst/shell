# Dependencies and artwork

## CTLST code

CTLST-authored source, configuration and documentation are licensed under
[Apache-2.0](LICENSE). Preserve [NOTICE](NOTICE) in redistributions.

## External dependencies

Sway, GTK, Waybar, libinput, Mako, Foot and the other packages listed in
[dependencies/](dependencies/) are installed separately. Their code, libraries
and fonts retain their upstream licenses and are not included in this
repository. No keyboard fork, firmware or device image is bundled.

See the [dependency guide](docs/DEPENDENCIES.md) for package roles and
[component inventory](packages/CORE-CONTENTS.md) for the install boundary.

## Artwork

The wallpapers and original SVG icons are project-created/generated artwork.
They are offered under Apache-2.0 to the extent rights exist and are held by
Vladimir Kovalchuk. This does not grant rights to third-party names or marks.

Report unclear origin or missing notices to the maintainer before
redistributing an affected file.
